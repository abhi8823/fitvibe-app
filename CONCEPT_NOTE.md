# Project Concept Note: FitVibe.AI

**Project Title / Application Name:** FitVibe – Personal AI Diet & Workout Coach  
**Target URL:** [To be pasted after AWS deployment]  
**LLM Model & API:** Google Gemini API (`gemini-2.5-flash`)

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Objective
Many people struggle to maintain a healthy lifestyle because standard diet and workout charts are generic and do not adapt to individual bodily metrics, food restrictions, or active schedules. Personal trainers and nutritionists are expensive and not accessible 24/7. 

**Objective:**  
To build and deploy a responsive, AI-native web application that acts as an elite, 24/7 personal health advisor. By collecting a user's precise physical details (age, weight, height, gender), diet patterns (vegetarian, vegan, keto), and fitness goals, **FitVibe** drafts an optimized daily meal and training program. The app also features a live chat coach for real-time fitness inquiries (e.g., exercise forms, recipes, rest-day strategies).

---

## 2. Target User & Use Cases
- **Working Professionals:** Need efficient home or gym workout blueprints that fit into tight calendars.
- **Fitness Beginners:** Require guidance on macro splits, calories, and simple exercise regimens without feeling overwhelmed.
- **Diet-Restricted Individuals:** Vegans, vegetarians, or keto followers seeking balanced, protein-rich meal recommendations that align with their fitness objectives.
- **Fitness Enthusiasts:** Seeking immediate, data-backed answers to niche coaching questions.

---

## 3. Key Features
- **Aesthetic Metrics Console:** Interactive sliders for real-time BMI estimation and Harris-Benedict daily calorie calculations directly on the client side.
- **Interactive AI Blueprint Generator:** Streams a comprehensive 7-day personalized workout schedule and nutrition chart customized for the user's specific diet and fitness targets.
- **Dynamic 24/7 FitVibe Chat Coach:** Real-time conversational interface where the coach acts on the user's metrics context to provide personalized guidance.
- **One-Click Print Optimization:** Native print support so users can export their generated 7-day routine to a physical sheet or PDF.

---

## 4. Expected User Experience (UX) & Outcomes
- **Wow Factor:** High-fidelity glassmorphic dark interface with neon accents, custom interactive sliders, and animated typing feedback.
- **No-Delay Interaction:** Live-streaming answers (progressive text loading) so the user gets responses instantly, word-by-word, rather than waiting for a complete backend buffer.
- **Actionable Guidance:** Easy-to-read tables and bullet points instead of long, discouraging text blocks.
- **Outcomes:** Increased user motivation, clear diet structure, safe exercise execution, and a clear roadmap to reach fitness milestones.
